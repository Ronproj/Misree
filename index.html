Why "Misree"

Named for the sugar that sweetens the cup.

Misri is crystallized rock sugar, stirred into chai and passed around after meals across South Asia. Misree Chai brings that ritual to Jersey City and Jackson Heights, alongside the milk teas, crepes and bowls our neighbors keep coming back for.
